# SQUATCH LIFE — The Meeting: Outcomes & Script

> **Legacy Initiation concept, preserved for character/story review.** This
> document treats Initiation as a prologue and turns a human Prospect into a
> sasquatch. That conflicts with the current chapter order and with the
> Squatchfather/Motel portrayal of Prospect. It is not campaign canon until the
> character alignment in [`CHARACTER-ALIGNMENT.md`](./CHARACTER-ALIGNMENT.md)
> is approved.

> **Review copy.** Nothing in here is wired into the game yet except what's
> already shipped (the ceremony flow). This is the writing pass for the four
> outcomes, in the intended voice, for sign-off before implementation.
>
> **The voice, in one paragraph:** HBO-grade. People bleed like people, swear
> like adults, and die badly. But the comedy is *deadpan*, not wacky — the
> Stanley Parable register the apartment scene already nails ("That is a
> normal amount of nothing"). Nobody in the Circle thinks anything unusual is
> happening. The horror is that the evening has an agenda and the agenda has
> bullet points, one of which is bullets. The jokes never wink. The gore never
> flinches.

## OUTCOME 1 — SILVER (the ceremony, rewritten)

Same flow as shipped. New words. Full script below; `[...]` are stage notes.

### The walk in

Objective text: `Follow the firelight.` Then, as the trees open:

> **NARRATOR CARD (subtle, bottom of screen):** The card said BRING NOTHING.
> You have brought, at most, nothing. Good start.

*(If arriving `stoned` or `merry` from the apartment, one extra line:)*

> **NARRATOR:** You are not as level as you think you are. Neither are the
> trees. Keep both facts to yourself.

### The line

Four strangers stand in a row like the last four beers in a fridge nobody
trusts. The purple light hums on the dirt. Objective: `Stand in the light.`

> **NARRATOR:** There is a spot marked out for you on the ground. That is
> either hospitality or forensics. Stand in it.

### The speech

**BOOSKIBRO:** Brothers. Sisters. Silverbacks of the Circle.

**BOOSKIBRO:** Shut the fuck up. *(nobody was talking)* Thank you.

**BOOSKIBRO:** Before the bandanas, there was a fire. Before this fire, there
were FIVE. Five men and women who walked out of their apartments, their
leases, their little lives — into these pines. The forest asked them a
question it asks everyone. They were the first ones to answer it out loud.
*(slam)*

**BOOSKIBRO:** People say we're a club. A club has a newsletter. People say
we're a gang. A gang has *ambitions*. We are a CIRCLE. A circle has no
beginning, no end, and no fucking exit.

**LOU SPUTTHOLE:** What Booski means is: welcome. This is a family. A large,
damp family with one rule, and the rule has teeth.

**LOU SPUTTHOLE:** Also, housekeeping — whoever keeps leaving cans at the fire
pit, the raccoons are organizing. I've seen the little one with the clipboard.
This is how it starts.

**BOOSKIBRO:** ENOUGH. Prospects. Five of you. Five founders. The forest
enjoys a rhyme. Your first trial is not of the body... but of the MIND.
*(slam)*

### The Question

**BOOSKIBRO:** Prospect One. Step forward.

*(Prospect One steps forward. He's wearing a rust-colored shirt he almost
certainly ironed for this.)*

**BOOSKIBRO:** Who are the five founding members of the Silver Sasquatches?

**PROSPECT ONE:** Okay. Okay okay okay. Booski... Lou... uh. Bigfoot?
Garfield?? ...the GEICO Gecko???

*(A pause the length of a funeral.)*

**LOU SPUTTHOLE:** The gecko. He said the gecko.

**BOOSKIBRO:** WRONG. *(slam)*

*(SNOW hands his beer to ERICAN without being asked. Walks out of the
semicircle unhurried, like a man asked to feed a meter. Draws. Eight rounds.
The first two put Prospect One down; the other six are policy. What's left in
the rust-colored shirt stops being a person and starts being a warning. The
crowd's reaction is the joke: there is no reaction. HOGMAMA checks her nails.)*

**LOU SPUTTHOLE:** ...Anyway.

**BOOSKIBRO:** Prospect Two. Same question.

**LOU SPUTTHOLE:** No pressure. Well. Some pressure. A specific, recently
demonstrated, still-cooling amount of pressure.

*(THE QUIZ. Options shuffled:)*
1. Booski, Lou Sputthole, Rippinflow, The Shubenator, Deathmegatron ✓
2. Booski, Lou Sputthole, Bigfoot, Garfield, the GEICO Gecko
3. Booski, Snow, Hogmama, Erican, and two raccoons in a coat

**On correct — BOOSKIBRO:** CORRECT. Myself. Lou Sputthole. Rippinflow. The
Shubenator. Deathmegatron. The FIVE. *(slam)*

**LOU SPUTTHOLE:** Somebody did the reading. Somebody gets to keep their
fluids on the inside.

**BOOSKIBRO:** The mind is sharp. Now we find out what the meat is made of.
Clear the line. THE GAUNTLET. *(slam)*

### The Gauntlet

Objective: `ENDURE. Raise a hand and join Prospect One.`

The circle closes. The beating is real — blood flecks with the fur... with the
*shirt fibers* now, teeth optional. Barks fly while they work you (see NPC
doc): nothing angry, which is worse. Deathmegatron apologizes mid-hook.
Somewhere around half health:

> **NARRATOR:** They are being gentle. It's important you know that. This is
> the version with restraint.

They stop at a fifth of your health, on Booski's word:

**BOOSKIBRO:** ENOUGH. *(the fists stop mid-air, all at once, like a shift
whistle went)*

**BOOSKIBRO:** Look at it. Still standing. Leaking from four places and
STANDING. The Circle sees you, prospect.

### The Roar / The Timber

**BOOSKIBRO:** Trial three. THE ROAR. Every animal in these woods knows our
voice. Tonight it learns yours. Empty your lungs or empty the slot — we have
more prospects.

*(You roar. The fire stands up. Something three ridges over decides to be
somewhere else.)*

**LOU SPUTTHOLE:** Hm. Felt that in the fillings.

**BOOSKIBRO:** Last trial. THE TIMBER. That deadfall has been lying there
since before you had a lease. Nobody will move it. Nobody but you. Break it,
and I'll tell you what it was for. *(slam)*

*(You break it in three. He never tells you what it was for. That's the bit —
if you talk to him at the party about it: "It was for that.")*

### The Anointing

**BOOSKIBRO:** *(close now, off the stage, pelt dripping firelight)* You took
the Circle's fists and kept your hands down. You gave the forest your voice.
You turned a log into a rumor of a log.

**BOOSKIBRO:** I'm going to tell you the secret now. The one the mud and the
bandana and the fucking mystery is for. Come here.

**BOOSKIBRO:** *(quiet, almost kind)* You walked into this clearing a man.

**BOOSKIBRO:** Walk out a SQUATCH. *(slam — the light swallows you; when it
lets go, you are nine feet of silver and the scream you were halfway through
has become something the county will file reports about)*

> **NARRATOR:** So that's what the pelt was. That's what all the pelts were.

→ **CUT TO: THE PARTY.**

## OUTCOME 2 — THE PARTY ("The Den")

The reward scene, same clearing, one hour later. The stage is now a bar.
Somebody has backed a pickup to the tree line with a keg in the bed. The
bonfire is twice the size because the Timber log is *in it* ("I'll tell you
what it was for"). Reuses the apartment's vice stack — beer, the bong,
cigarettes — with the same drunk/high camera feel ported over.

**The dark bit, played completely straight:** Prospect One is still where he
fell. Someone has put a red Solo cup in his hand and a bandana on him.
Post-mortem membership. There's an interaction prompt:

> `E — Pour one out for Prospect One`
> **NARRATOR:** He got in. Technically. There's a lesson there and everyone
> here is too drunk to phrase it.

**Structure:** no objectives, no timer. A door out (the tree line) works
whenever. It's the apartment philosophy outdoors: the party IS the game.
Things to do:

| Thing | What it is |
|---|---|
| The keg | Apartment beer system, communal. Six deep and the fire develops opinions. |
| The bong circle | SHUBES runs it from a camp chair. World slows, camera lags — ported from apartment `highs.js`. |
| The toast | Booski on the truck bed. Scripted set piece, below. |
| Log toss | RIPPINFLOW's minigame with the leftover Timber chunks. Timing bar. |
| The wall of founders | Five crooked portraits nailed to a pine. One is a raccoon. Nobody explains. |
| Talk to everyone | The NPC system's showcase — every member has party dialog keyed to what you did to/with them tonight. See `docs/NPC-SYSTEM.md`. |

**Booski's toast** (he's four beers in; the gravitas keeps slipping):

**BOOSKIBRO:** QUIET. Quiet. Tonight the Circle grew by one. And shrank by
one. *(gestures at Prospect One with his cup)* The math is called nature,
and nature is called — Lou, what's it called —

**LOU SPUTTHOLE:** Attrition.

**BOOSKIBRO:** NATURE IS CALLED ATTRITION. To the new blood!

**EVERYONE:** TO THE NEW BLOOD!

**BOOSKIBRO:** *(sitting down heavily)* And to the old blood. *(quietly, into
the cup)* Wherever we buried it.

**End of night:** walk to the tree line, hold E. Ending card:

> **SILVER.** You came here from an apartment with a wobble in the door
> handle. You are leaving as an apex predator with a bandana and a hangover
> scheduled for noon. There is no going back. Your lease, ironically, also
> says that.

**AGENT DOYLE:

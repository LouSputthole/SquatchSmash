# The Initiation — Outcomes, Party & NPC System

> **Approved pre-Initiation canon with a frozen current ending.** Tony
> Squatchtana and every Circle member present as human before the verdict.
> Initiation is the chapter endpoint, the named face photos are authoritative,
> the five founders are locked, and Prospect One's execution remains. After the
> user tests the current scene, the approved rewrite will judge campaign
> accomplishments, execute every failed rival prospect, and transform Tony plus
> all recognized family members into literal sasquatches. Those are the only
> locked ending changes. The party, FBI/rat route, ghost variants, and other
> alternate endings below are archived proposals, not approved implementation.

This is future writing, not the active route. The production spine must first
complete apartment → Bada Bing One → Squatchfather → Beef Run → Bada Bing Two
→ Jerky Motel → apartment/big-night call. Until that route is connected and
the current ceremony is user-tested, Initiation is available only from the
save-isolated `preview.html` launcher.

The material below is a writing archive for later review. It is not an active
build order and must not override the current Initiation freeze or the canon
listed above.

Tone target: **dark comedy, HBO gore, Stanley-Parable deadpan.** The apartment
scene already set the register — dry, lowercase, unbothered, the joke dies the
second anyone sounds scared. We keep that voice in your head and the narrator's
mouth, and then we let the *forest* be as ugly as it wants. The comedy is that
nobody in the clearing thinks any of this is unusual.

Two voices do the heavy lifting and they must never blur:

- **THE NARRATOR** — your interior voice, carried over from the apartment. Dry,
  present-tense, faintly disappointed in you. Never frightened. Describes
  atrocity in the same tone it used to describe the fridge.
- **THE CIRCLE** — Booskibro, Big Uncle Lou, the crew. Loud, ceremonial, genuinely warm even
  while doing something monstrous. They love you. That's what makes it work.

### A · THE BROTHER  *(best outcome → the party)*

You answered right. You ate the beating and never swung. You roared. You put
the log down. Booskibro comes off the stage.

> **BOOSKIBRO:** You walked in here a prospect. Soft. Renting. A person who *owns a
> colander.*
>
> **BOOSKIBRO:** Tonight you walked into the fire a prospect and you're walking out
> the other side of it something the forest actually has a use for.
>
> **BIG UNCLE LOU SPUTTHOLE:** We're all so proud of you. Genuinely. I might cry. I won't,
> but I might.
>
> **NARRATOR:** Somewhere behind you, the man who couldn't name the founders is

### B · THE RAT  *(FBI route → you die)*

Set up in the apartment: at some point on Tuesday, a message that isn't from the
crew. Different colour, no lowercase, no warmth. (Reuses the chat monitor.)

> **UNKNOWN:** Mr. [surname]. We know about Wednesday. We know what they are.
>
> **UNKNOWN:** We've been trying to get someone inside the Silver Sasquatches
> for three years. Every one we send comes back different, or doesn't come back.
>
> **UNKNOWN:** You're already invited. That makes you the most valuable asset the
> Bureau has ever had in the tree line. There's a firearm taped under your
> nightstand drawer. It was there before you moved in. Don't ask.
>
> **UNKNOWN:** Get close to Booskibro. That's all. When the moment's right, you'll
> know. Take the head off the animal and the body stops.
>
> **NARRATOR:** You do not remember agreeing to any of this. You are, however,
> now holding a gun. The tutorial for how to feel about that is not included.

**What changes:** you carry the pistol *into* the initiation (the same gun rig I
already built for the execution — it just changes hands). A cold **RESOLVE**
meter replaces the health bar in the corner. Everything you endure — the
Gauntlet, the Roar — you're not enduring for the bandana anymore. You're waiting.

The tell is the writing. Every warm line the Circle gives you now lands wrong,
because you know what's taped to your leg:

> **BOOSKIBRO:** *(mid-beating, delighted)* THERE it is! He's not even covering up!
> That's a *believer!*
>
> **NARRATOR:** He thinks you've transcended the pain. You've stopped feeling it
> because you're doing arithmetic. Six shots gets Booskibro. Then Big Uncle Lou, who's closer
> than he should be. Then the tree line, and forty minutes of running, and a
> life afterward you have genuinely not thought about.

**The kill window** comes at the anointing — the one moment Booskibro is off the
stage, unarmed, an arm's length away, calling you *son*. The game hands you the
prompt: **[hold] RAISE THE GUN.** Two beats to decide.

**Then it goes wrong, because it was always going to.** Two drafts of the death,
pick the flavour:

- **B1 — THE FASTEST DRAW IS BEHIND YOU.** The gun clears the holster. You get
  the barrel level with Booskibro's grin. And *Rippinflow* — who has said maybe
  four words all night, who you clocked as the quiet one — puts a round through
  the back of your skull before your finger finds the trigger. The Circle knew.
  The Circle *always* knows. That's the whole reason the initiation exists.
  > **NARRATOR:** The last thing you see is Booskibro's face fall — not fear.
  > *Disappointment.* He really did think you were the one.
  > **BOOSKIBRO:** *(genuinely, softly, over your body)* ...aw. He was *good,* too.
  > **LOU:** They're always good. That's the tragedy of it.

- **B2 — YOU GET HIM.** The gun works. Booskibro goes down with the top of his head
  redecorating the banner, and for one clean second you are the man who killed a
  legend. Then you remember there are ten of them and one of you, and the forest
  does the rest — not fast, not merciful. The screen doesn't cut away.
  > **NARRATOR:** You killed the animal. Nobody told you the body doesn't stop.
  > Nobody told you the body has *knives.*
  > **HOG MAMA:** *(unhurried, wiping something on her jeans)* Get the tarp. Not
  > the good tarp.

Recommendation: **ship B1 as the default,** offer B2 as a rare branch if you
land a headshot in the two-beat window — reward the trigger discipline with a
worse death. Very us.

### C · THE WASHOUT  *(fail a trial → you die, many ways)*

This replaces the current soft "fail → retry" screen with something with teeth.
Failing is no longer a do-over prompt; it's a death, and *which* death depends on
*how* you failed. Same "TRY AGAIN" button underneath — the comedy is the
cheerful menu-button under the corpse.

Each is a short, specific, nasty beat. The Circle is never angry. That's the
horror and the joke: they'll kill you the way you'd put down a dog that bit,
sadly, and correctly, and then go back to the fire.

| Failure | Death | The line |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrong founders** *(already built as the execution)* | Eight rounds, on the spot, from the nearest member. | **BOOSKIBRO:** "Wrong." *(no second word)* |
| **Swing back in the Gauntlet** | You hit a brother. The ring stops being playful. It closes. You go under a dozen boots and don't come up. Camera stays at ground level. | **NARRATOR:** "You raised a hand to family. The family has a policy on that." |
| **Don't roar / roar too soft** | The forest was supposed to learn your voice. It didn't. So it takes the voice — Deathmegatron's thumb finds your windpipe and the screen goes to the wet dark. | **DEATHMEGATRON:** "Nothing. He's got *nothing* in him." |
| **Miss the log / hit a brother during the Timber** | The great log was a mercy. You wasted it. They put you under it — the same log, across the throat, two of them standing on the ends. | **LOU:** "He couldn't even do the *easy* one." *(sincerely sad)* |
| **Run for the tree line at any point** | You get twenty yards. Rippinflow was already there. He's always already there. | **NARRATOR:** "There is no edge of the clearing. There is only how far Rippinflow lets you get." |

The dark-comedy load-bearing beam: **the narrator eulogizes you like it's your
own dry apartment voice noticing you left the tap on.**

> **NARRATOR:** So that's it. Thirty-seven hours of prep, a clean shirt, and you
> died in a forest because you couldn't remember the Shubenator. There's another
> meeting next Wednesday. You will not be at it.

### D · THE FOURTH  *(pick one)*

**D1 — THE GHOST (Stanley Parable exit).** At the kill window, or at any quiz
prompt, you can just... not. Don't raise the gun. Don't answer. Walk backward
into the tree line while everyone waits for you to do the thing. Nobody stops
you — because refusing *is* an answer, and it's the one nobody's brave enough to
give. You walk home. The apartment is exactly as you left it. The card is still
on the corkboard. There's another meeting next Wednesday. Neither the Circle nor
the Bureau ever contacts you again. You got away with the only thing worse than
dying: nothing.
> **NARRATOR:** You are the first man in the history of the Silver Sasquatches to
> neither join them nor die. There is no word for what you are. You go home. You
> own a colander.

**D2 — THE INFORMANT WHO LIVED.** The FBI route, but you never take the shot —
you complete the initiation *as a rat,* wire intact, and walk out an actual
member feeding the Bureau. Best/worst of both: you're at the party (Outcome A's
party) but every warm line now has a second meaning, the RESOLVE meter is still
in the corner, and the ending card is a slow, awful realization that you can
never leave either family, and both will eventually find out.
> **NARRATOR:** You are a made member of the Silver Sasquatches and a federal
> informant. You have two families now. Both of them kill people who lie to
> them. You did the reading. You know how this ends. You go to the party anyway.

Recommendation: **D1** is the cleaner comedy and cheaper to build. **D2** is the
better story but doubles the party's writing (every line needs a paranoid
variant) and needs the RESOLVE system fully wired. If we're being ambitious, D2.
If we're shipping, D1.

## THE PARTY  *(Outcome A's reward)*

The apartment scene is a flat you can't leave. The party is the inverse: a
clearing you've *earned,* full of people, where the only goal is to be there.
Reuse everything.

| Apartment system | Party reuse |
|---|---|
| **Beer ×6 / whiskey** | A keg on a stump and a bottle going round the fire. Same drunk model. |
| **The bong / weed** | A joint doing laps of the circle — hold [E] when it reaches you. Same slow-time effect. |
| **Mushrooms** | Someone's got caps. Same 95-second delayed bend. The forest is a *much* better place to be tripping than the flat, and the visuals know it. |
| **Narrator** | Same voice, now describing a party instead of an empty room. |
| **Interaction system** | Same look-at-and-hold. Stations become people and props around the fire. |
| **Drunk / highs camera** | Unchanged. Stack them. |

**The party has no fail state and no clock pressure.** It's a victory lap you
can stay in as long as you want. The "ending" is chosen the same way the
apartment's is — from the state you're in when you finally decide to sit down at
the fire and let it fade. Drunk ending, stoned ending, tripping ending, the
stone-sober "you actually just enjoyed it" ending.

What makes it worth building is the **NPC system** — because the reward isn't the
beer, it's that eleven specific people are *glad you're here* and each one says
so differently.

## THE NPC SYSTEM  *(the real ask — fully fleshed)*

Every Circle member is a real human with a fixed **archetype,** a **standing** toward
you that moved during the initiation, a **state** (sober→wrecked, reusing the
drunk model), and a **station** at the party (what they're doing). When you look
at one and they clock you, they pull a line from the bank that matches
*archetype × standing × state.* That's the whole engine. It's small and it will
feel enormous.

### The data model

```js
// One entry per crew member. Faces already exist in assets/faces/.
const NPC = {
  id: 'booski',
  name: 'BOOSKIBRO',
  face: 'assets/faces/booski.png',
  archetype: 'PATRIARCH',     // drives cadence + what they care about
  standing: 0,                // -100..100, moved by your run (see below)
  state: 'sober',             // sober | merry | drunk | wrecked | stoned | tripping
  station: 'fire',            // where they are + what they're doing
  suspicion: 0,               // 0..100, only ever > 0 on the RAT/D2 routes
  linesSaid: new Set(),       // never repeat a line in one session
};
```

**Standing** starts at a value set by *how the initiation went* and drifts up as
you drink/smoke with people. Key moments that move it:

- Took the whole Gauntlet without flinching → everyone +.
- Roared loud (long hold) → the loud ones (Ape, Deathmegatron) love it, +big.
- Hesitated on the quiz → the sharp ones (Booskibro, Big Uncle Lou) note it, small −.
- On the RAT route, *every* interaction quietly raises **suspicion** on a random
  nearby NPC, and high suspicion flips their party lines from warm to probing.

**State** is just the drunk model's number bucketed, read off each NPC's own
running total — they're drinking too, on their own timers. A wrecked member and
a sober one give completely different lines from the same bank. This is free
content: the party gets funnier the longer you stay because everyone's sliding
down their own curve.

### The archetypes  *(cadence + concerns, mapped to the real crew)*

| Archetype | Who | How they talk | What they say to a new brother |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PATRIARCH** | Booskibro | Ceremonial even drunk. Everything's a proclamation. Warm as a furnace. | Keeps *toasting* you. Tells the story of your Gauntlet to people who were standing right there. |
| **LIEUTENANT** | Big Uncle Lou Sputthole | The translator. Softens Booskibro, roasts gently, means all of it. Airport energy — permanently in transit, emotionally present. | "What he means is welcome. What I mean is: don't touch the good tarp, you'll learn what it's for." |
| **THE QUIET ONE** | Rippinflow | Four words a night. On the RAT route, the scariest man alive. At the party, unnervingly kind, which is worse. | *(long pause)* "You did good." *(that's the whole conversation, and it's the one you'll remember)* |
| **THE ROASTER** | Ape | Never says a nice thing directly; every insult is love. The apartment's "shubes cried" energy. | "You took thirty punches and cried less than Shubes did. That's the bar. You cleared the bar." |
| **THE CRIER** | Shubenator / Shubes | Defensive, sincere, deeply moved and hates that he is. Denies crying while crying. | "I'm not — it's the smoke. It's a smoky fire. Welcome, man. *(sniff)* It's the smoke." |
| **THE MUSCLE** | Deathmegatron | Few words, all physical. Communicates in nods and headlocks. The one who'd have choked you out if you'd failed the Roar, now your biggest fan. | *(a nod. a beer pressed into your hand hard enough to bruise.)* "Voice on this guy." |
| **THE MATRIARCH** | Hog Mama | Practical, unbothered, has seen worse and cleaned it up. Runs logistics on everything including the murders. | "You eat? There's dogs on the fire. Real ones. Don't make it weird." |
| **THE GRIEVANCE** | Irish | Always mid-complaint about something procedural and unrelated. Never once addresses the murder or the party directly. | "Nobody has answered my question about the eggs. You're new — *you* answer it. What is the egg situation." |
| **THE UTILITY** | Erican, Gratin, Captain Lou Sasole, Snow | Background crew, lighter banks, keep the circle populated and drinking. | Toasts, "welcome brother," a story about their own initiation that's clearly worse than yours. |

### The reaction rules  *(look-at → line)*

When you put the crosshair on an NPC and hold [E] (same interaction system):

1. **Filter the bank** by `archetype`, then by `standing` tier
   (`stranger / prospect / brother / beloved`), then by `state`.
2. **Drop anything in `linesSaid`.** Never repeat inside a session.
3. **RAT/D2 override:** if this NPC's `suspicion > 60`, pull from the *probing*
   bank instead — same warmth on the surface, a hook underneath.
4. Speak it. Bump `standing` a touch for engaging. On the RAT route, bump a
   random nearby NPC's `suspicion`.

**Ambient barks** run without you: NPCs talk *to each other* on a loop when
you're near, so the party sounds alive whether or not you're working the room.
(Ape roasting Shubes forever is a renewable resource.)

### Sample bank — Booskibro (PATRIARCH), "brother" standing

```
sober:   "There he is. THE MAN OF THE HOUR. Somebody get him a drink he can't refuse."
merry:   "I've inducted forty men. I remember maybe six. You, I'll remember."
drunk:   "You know what the founders had that nobody has anymore? *(long pause)* ...I forget. But you've got it too."
wrecked: "You're my son now. Legally? No. Forest-legally? Absolutely. Don't test it."
```

### Sample bank — Booskibro, RAT route, suspicion high

```
"You're quiet. My prospects are never quiet. What are you *thinking* about over there?"
"Come here. Closer. ...No, closer. I want to see your eyes when I say welcome."
"Rippinflow likes you. Rippinflow doesn't like anyone. Makes me wonder what he sees."
```

That last line is the system doing horror for free: a warm host complimenting
you, and the compliment is that the killer is watching you.

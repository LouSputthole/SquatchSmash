# Squatch Life domain language

## Radio

- **Station** — 97.8 The Squatch: its clock-driven shows, records, links,
  commercial, tape, and the single Day One meeting notice.
- **Receiver** — a physical radio in a scene. Apartment, Bing car, and NO WAKE
  boat receivers have separate power preferences but share Station progress.
- **Running order** — the durable position through talk, links, records,
  commercial, tape, and notice slots. It continues across scenes and reloads.
- **Bulletin** — a chapter-specific news interruption. A Bulletin is recorded
  as heard only when it begins airing, and it never replays after save/load.
- **Authored set** — the Bada Bing DJ record chosen by story visit: visit one
  and visit two have distinct records; a player request can replace the deck.

The meeting notice expires with Day One. Later references to the Initiation or
the big night are separate story beats and must never reactivate that notice.
